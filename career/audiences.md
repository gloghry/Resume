# Audience profiles

Profiles guide selection, not proof of qualification or a claim of applicant
intent. Requirement-specific evidence always takes precedence over tag matching.

| Profile ID | Audience and emphasis | Candidate fact IDs | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| firmware-embedded | Firmware or embedded engineering: C, communications, hardware integration and debugging | hy-employment, hy-middleware, hy-tools, embedded-coursework, wsu-degree | Label coursework; no unconfirmed robotics, protocol, or safety claims |
| application-development | Application engineering: C#/.NET tools, diagnostic workflows, Python pipelines | hy-tools, seasalt-capstone, hy-employment, wsu-degree | Do not infer web, cloud, architecture, or deployment experience |
| systems-os | Systems-oriented roles: hardware/software boundaries, C, queues, interrupts | hy-middleware, embedded-coursework, hy-tools, wsu-degree | Academic RTOS evidence is not professional OS/kernel expertise; unsupported requirements are gaps |
| technical-management | Technical and operational leadership: technical ownership, retail supervision, mentoring and documentation | hy-tools, music-world, hy-middleware, wsu-degree | Technical ownership does not establish engineering people management or budget authority |
| nonengineering-operations | Operations or retail management: store operations, staff development, procedural consistency | music-world, hy-tools, wsu-degree | Explain tools in terms of supported workflows; no invented performance metrics |
| wsu-metm | WSU METM application: technical foundation, operational context, supervision, and communication | wsu-degree, hy-tools, hy-middleware, music-world, seasalt-capstone | Provisional selection profile; verify supplied program requirements; no assumption of admission, goals, or program criteria |

Suggested ordering: engineering profiles lead with relevant technical experience;
management/program profiles connect technical work to operational use and give
Music World meaningful space. Keep actual employers, dates, titles, and academic
labels intact even when section order changes. Target one or two readable pages
for the application in #16; length elsewhere follows the supplied requirements.
