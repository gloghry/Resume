# Résumé and career work

For career research or résumé tailoring, read `career/README.md`,
`career/facts.md`, `career/review.md`, `career/audiences.md`,
`career/tailoring.md`, and `career/privacy.md` first.

Use only eligible, source-backed facts. Repository evidence is an applicant
statement, not independent verification. Never promote review items or interests
into demonstrated experience. Do not infer metrics, titles, dates, proficiency,
management authority, or employer permission from tags or audience preferences.

Keep personal application inputs, contact details, sensitive evidence, and draft
PDFs in ignored `private/` or outside the repository. Public issues, commits,
build logs, artifacts, and releases are publication surfaces. Review the exact
staged diff before committing. Do not change existing résumé versions or the
release workflow merely to tailor a new résumé.
